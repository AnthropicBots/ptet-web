Student Visa Information

Everything you need to know about USA student visas

Student Visa

F-1 Student Visa is the most common visa for international students at U.S. universities.

Key Requirements

  • Form I-20 from a SEVP-certified institution.
  • Proof of English proficiency (PTE, TOEFL, IELTS).
  • Financial support provided to cover tuition and living expenses.
  • Valid passport and completed visa application (DS-160).

Processing Time

Apply for your visa after receiving your I-20. Processing times vary by embassy, check your local U.S. consulate for current wait times.

Visa Validity

Students are legally allowed to remain in the U.S. for the full duration of the academic program as long as they maintain active student status.
