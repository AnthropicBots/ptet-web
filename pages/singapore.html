Why Study in Singapore?

Singapore is home to many of the world's highest-ranked universities, offering unmatched academic excellence, cutting-edge research opportunities, diverse cultural experiences, and strong industry connections. Students gain access to world-class faculty, internships with leading companies, and excellent career prospects after graduation.

Student Visa Information

Everything you need to know about Singaporean student visas

Student Visa

Student's Pass issued via Singapore's Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA).

Key Requirements

  • Offer of admission from a registered institution in Singapore.
  • Completion of the Student's Pass application via the SOLAR system.
  • Proof of financial capability to cover tuition and living expenses.
  • Medical examination clearances.

Processing Time

Applications should be submitted at least 2 months before the course begins through ICA's online portal.
